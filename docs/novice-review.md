# Novice landlord review

Reviewer persona: a first-time landlord with a high-school education, renting out one basement unit, comfortable with a phone but not with finance or legal words.

## What they said, and what changed

### Website
| # | Feedback | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Half these words mean nothing to me" (T776, N4, GIC, depreciation, cost base, tribunal, arrears). | Plain name first, code second ("official notice for unpaid rent (N4)", "rental part of your tax return (CRA form T776)"). Hard words have a dotted underline; tap for a one-line meaning. |
| 2 | "Get started or See it in action, which one?" | One main button, **Start free**. Second button reads **Try a demo first, no sign-up**. |
| 3 | "I made an account. How do I get back in?" | **Sign in** in the menu, a Sign in page, Google or email link. |
| 4 | "Is this going to cost me?" | "Free for 1 rental unit · No card needed" under the headline; cost added to the FAQ. |
| 5 | "Is Gold a paid level?" | "It's always free: these are not paid levels." |
| 6 | "ON, BC, N4 → L1 → L9?" | Province card shows full form names with the code as a small tag, and "Rules set by …". |
| 7 | "Why does it want my bank? Can it take my money?" | Reassurance right at the bank step: "Keyholder can only look, never move your money. Or skip this." Added to FAQ. |
| 8 | Long sentences. | Copy rewritten to about a grade-8 reading level. |

### Signup questions
| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Where do your tenants live? | Where is your rental property? |
| What's your setup? ("doors") | What are you renting out? |
| How does rent reach you today? | How do your tenants pay you? |
| What makes you dread the 1st of the month? | What's the hardest part of being a landlord? |
| What worries you at tax time? ("deduct", "repair or renovation") | What's hard about taxes for you? |
| What would make this worth it for you? | What do you want help with most? |

Each question now shows "Question N of 6" and a one-line reason ("why we ask"). The long plan letter became a short checklist, followed by **Save your plan**: Continue with Google, Continue with email, or try the sample first.
